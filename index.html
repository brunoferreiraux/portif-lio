About Me

Business-driven design, research and systems

I'm a UI/UX Product Designer with 17 years of experience in Design, UX/UI, Digital Products and Digital Strategy, creating user-centered solutions for companies like Porto Seguro, Bradesco, Santander, Americanas and Volkswagen.

I have experience across the full design cycle — from user research to high-fidelity interface delivery — with strong focus on Design Ops, Design Systems and agile methodologies (Scrum and Kanban), collaborating closely with Product Managers, developers and cross-functional teams.

I also work in regulated environments (projects for SUSEP) and healthcare products, always balancing usability, efficiency and business requirements. More recently, I've been applying Artificial Intelligence to accelerate design processes and boost team productivity.
